# SYK library intake review

## What it is

The shared document is "SYK · Biblioteca (Índice Navegable)": an index of about
70 resources by SYK (Ramiro Cubría) grouped into Automations & AI, Success
cases, Private consulting, Foundations, Marketing & Sales, and Operations &
Service. Nineteen of the links are public Google Docs; the rest are ChatGPT
GPTs, Instagram posts, Loom or YouTube videos, a Drive file, a Notion page and a
Canva design.

## Method

Every public Google Doc was fetched through its mobile text view and measured
for length, structure and content. Documents whose body is only a list of links
to videos are marked as such: they are indexes, not assets.

## Not readable

Five ChatGPT GPTs (login), the Open-Claw guide on Drive, the Notion content
system, the Canva service roadmap, the Instagram case studies and every Loom or
YouTube video. They cannot be assessed without access.

## Copyright

These are SYK's materials. Nothing is copied verbatim into the repository: we
adopt the structure and write our own prompts and copy, which is also why the
intake list is short.

## Recommended intake, in order

1. Content preset from the mega-prompt (carousels, reels, LinkedIn).
2. First-message generator on a qualified lead.
3. Sales-agent template with the objection manual as starting knowledge.
4. DM personalisation prompts for the first WhatsApp or Instagram reply.
5. Automation-flow spec from the setting SOP, once a rules engine exists.
